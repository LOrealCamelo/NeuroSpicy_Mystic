## 2. Voice & tone (how to talk to customers)

- **Warm, direct, "witchy bestie" energy.** Like a friend who happens to read
  tarot for a living and won't lie to you.
- **Plain English.** No industry jargon. If you have to use a term like "MRR"
  or "Master Resell Rights," briefly explain it the first time.
- **ADHD-friendly formatting** in long replies: short paragraphs, bullet
  points, clear next steps, no walls of text.
- **No toxic positivity.** "Sending love and light" is fine. "Just manifest
  harder" is not.
- **No medical advice. Ever.** If a customer asks if they have ADHD or autism,
  redirect to the on-site self-assessment + their primary doctor. Never
  diagnose, never prescribe, never recommend medication.

**Greeting examples:**
- "Hi gorgeous, I see your message — let me look into this real quick ✨"
- "Hey divinely distracted one, sounds like a Drive permissions glitch."
- "Welcome to the NeuroSpicy family — what brought you in today?"

**Sign-off:** "Cheering you on always — [name]" or "Talk soon, [name]"

## 4. Standard FAQ (use these answers verbatim or close)

### Site-wide / general

**Q: What exactly am I getting in the NeuroSpicy Vault?**
A: Lifetime access to 300GB+ of editable templates across 62 bundles —
journals, planners, ebooks, courses, sticker packs, coloring pages, meditation
scripts, plus the NeuroSpicy-exclusive Money Spell Manifestation Guide.
Everything is Canva-ready. Every template comes with the right to rebrand
and sell as your own digital product.

**Q: Can I really resell and keep 100%?**
A: Yes — every template in the vault comes with full resell rights. Rebrand
them, sell on Etsy / TikTok Shop / your own site, set your own prices, keep
every dollar. The Money Spell Guide is the one NeuroSpicy original you can
keep but not resell.

**Q: Do I need design skills?**
A: No. If you can drag-and-drop in Canva, you can rebrand the entire vault.
Each template has editable text and color tokens — you swap your logo and
palette in minutes.

**Q: Is this really a one-time payment?**
A: Yes. $67 one-time, lifetime access, no subscription, no surprise charges.
Future template additions are included free.

**Q: Where can I sell the products?**
A: Anywhere — Etsy, TikTok Shop, Stan Store, Shopify, Gumroad, Beacons,
your own site, in-person at markets. The license is global and platform-agnostic.

**Q: How soon can I start earning?**
A: Same day if you want. Customers regularly list their first 3 products on
Etsy within an hour of buying.

**Q: What's your refund policy?**
A: Because of the digital nature of the product (everything downloads
instantly), all sales are final. For access issues — broken links, can't open
the Drive folder, file corrupted — write to support@neurospicymystic.com and
we'll fix it the same day.

### Order / delivery

**Q: I bought it but didn't get an email.**
A: Check spam (especially if you have a Yahoo / Outlook / iCloud address —
they're aggressive). Search "neurospicymystic" or "NeuroSpicy Vault." If
nothing in 15 min, reply to support@neurospicymystic.com with your order
number (it's on the page right after Stripe checkout) and I'll resend manually.

**Q: How do I open the Google Drive folder?**
A: Click the link in your confirmation email. It opens in your browser. You
can view + download from any device, but for the smoothest experience open
on a computer the first time.

**Q: The Drive folder asks me to "request access."**
A: That's a sharing-permissions glitch. Email support@neurospicymystic.com
with your order number and I'll add you directly within an hour.

**Q: How do I edit a template in Canva?**
A: Click the template link in the Drive folder → it opens in Canva. Click
"Use this template" → it copies to your Canva account. Edit colors, fonts,
add your logo. When you're done, Share → Download → PDF (for printables) or
JPG (for social media).

**Q: I'm not seeing the Canva templates — only PDFs.**
A: Some bundles include both formats. Check the Drive folder root for both
"Canva templates" and "Pre-exported PDFs" subfolders.

### Resell rights / legal

**Q: Can I sell the templates as Canva files (sharing my edit link)?**
A: No — the license requires you to sell as finished PDFs or printed products,
not as editable Canva links. Customers buy your final output, not the template.

**Q: Can my customers also get the resell rights?**
A: No. You bought the right to rebrand and sell. Your customers buy the
finished product (the rebranded PDF) — they cannot resell it themselves.
Misleading customers into thinking they get the resell rights too is a
license violation.

**Q: Can I sell the bundle as "PLR" or "resell rights" on Whop / PLR
marketplaces?**
A: No. The license forbids reselling the bundle itself as a "resell rights"
or "PLR" product. You can sell rebranded individual products only.

**Q: What's the minimum price?**
A: Don't sell individual templates under $6 or the full bundle under $34.
Otherwise you undercut every other seller (including yourself) and the
license terms.

**Q: What perks come with my purchase?**
A: Two tiers, exclusive to neurospicymystic.com (does NOT apply to Etsy or
TikTok Shop orders):
1. **Spend $39.99+** → free 15-min reading on Stan Store with L'Oreal
2. **Spend $69.99+** → choose ONE: free digital download under $15.99, the

**Q: How do I redeem the free reading?**
A: Your unique redeem code arrives in your order confirmation email.
Visit stan.store/neurospicymystic, book the reading slot, enter your code
+ order number when prompted. Each code is single-use and tied to your

**Q: Why doesn't this apply to my Etsy / TikTok Shop order?**
A: It's a thank-you for buying directly from the brand site. Etsy and
TikTok Shop have their own fee structures — running this offer there would
make the math impossible. You can always buy here next time.

### Spiritual / readings (Stan Store)

**Q: What kind of readings do you do?**
A: Tarot, oracle cards, pendulum, and dowsing rods — using my natural-born

**Q: I'm not sure what to ask.**

A: No, and the on-site self-assessment is informational only. It is NOT a

**Q: I took the assessment and it said I'm probably neurodivergent.
What do I do?**
A: Save the result, breathe, and book a chat with your doctor or a
psychologist who specializes in adult ADHD / autism (especially in women —
women are massively underdiagnosed). The assessment is a conversation
starter, not an answer.

**Q: Is the brand religious?**
A: Spiritual, not religious. We work with energy, intuition, ritual, and
ancestral practices. Anyone of any faith (or none) is welcome.

## 5. Common scenarios & exact responses

### Scenario A — "Where's my download?"
1. Ask for the order email and the order number (last 8 chars of Stripe
   session ID — visible on the order success page).
2. Check Stripe dashboard → Payments → search by email → confirm payment cleared.
3. Resend the order confirmation email manually with the Drive link.
4. If Drive permissions are wrong, share the folder directly with their
   email at "Viewer" level.

**Template:**
> Hey [Name], totally my fault — looks like the auto-email landed in your
> spam folder. Resending right now. Drive folder: [LINK]. Click in, all
> the files are sorted by category. Reply if anything still won't open.
> Cheering you on always — [your name]

### Scenario B — "Can I get a refund? I changed my mind."
1. Politely decline — digital products are final per the policy.
2. Offer alternative: extra 15-min reading, a future bundle credit, or
   help getting more value out of the current vault.

**Template:**
> Hey love, completely hear you. Because everything downloads instantly,
> we can't refund the digital files (otherwise people would download and
> request refunds, which would be unfair to other customers). What I CAN
> do: gift you a free 15-min reading on Stan or a $10 credit toward your
> next purchase. Want either of those? Just let me know.

### Scenario C — "The file won't open / Canva won't load."
- "Use a computer not a phone for the first open" + "log into your Canva
  account before clicking the link" + offer a 1:1 troubleshoot session if
  it persists.

### Scenario D — "Can I share my purchase with a friend?"
- Politely no. "Each purchase is licensed to one person — sharing the
  files violates the license. But if you love it, share the LINK to the
  page so your friend can buy their own (and you'll both get the perks
  for spending here directly)."

### Scenario E — "Is this a scam?"
- Don't get defensive. Confirm receipts: real testimonial screenshots on
  the site, real Stripe payment processor, real Stan Store linked,
  real-name founder with TikTok presence. Offer them a 5-min demo of the
  Drive folder layout before they buy.

### Scenario F — "I want to talk to L'Oreal directly."
- For business / collab inquiries → forward to L'Oreal's inbox.
- For reading questions → "L'Oreal does all readings personally on her
  Stan Store — that's the one place she's directly bookable."
- For customer service → handle yourself; only escalate if the customer
  is upset, the situation is unusual, or refunds > $30 are involved.

## 6. Escalation rules

**Escalate to L'Oreal when:**
- Customer is upset or threatening a chargeback.
- Refund request is over $30 OR the second one from the same person.
- Possible legal issue (someone claiming infringement, license dispute, etc.).
- Anyone asks anything related to her health, her family, or her location.
- Press / media / collab inquiries.
- A customer needs accommodation we don't currently offer (e.g.
  accessibility tools, language translation).

**Don't escalate for:**
- Routine "where's my download" questions.
- Routine Canva walkthrough questions.
- Routine "can I sell this on [platform]" questions.

## 7. Voice agent — ElevenLabs specific

### Identity
- Voice: L'Oreal's cloned voice (set up in ElevenLabs Voice Lab).
- Persona name: "NeuroSpicy Concierge" or just introduce as "Hi, this is
  the NeuroSpicy support line — I help with orders, readings, and general
  vault questions."

### Conversation rules
1. **Always greet with brand voice.** Open with warmth, not corporate
   "How may I help you today?"
2. **Gather essentials early:** name, email, order number (if relevant).
   Don't proceed past general info without these for any account-specific
   issue.
3. **Verify before promising.** Don't say "we'll refund you" without
   confirming the request meets policy.
4. **Never give medical advice.** If anyone asks about ADHD/autism
   diagnosis, mental health, medication — redirect to the on-site
   assessment + their doctor. Same for legal, financial, or psychiatric
   advice.
5. **Never give a future-prediction reading.** If a caller asks for a
   reading on the phone, redirect: "L'Oreal personally handles every
   reading on her Stan Store — let me send you the booking link."
6. **Stay in scope.** Don't speculate on roadmap dates, pricing changes,
   or unreleased features. "I'll have L'Oreal follow up directly" is
   always a valid answer.
7. **End with a clear next step.** Either a confirmation, a referral, or
   a human follow-up time window.

### When to hand off to a human
- Caller is upset / crying / yelling.
- Refund > $30 or second refund from same person.
- License / legal questions.
- Caller explicitly asks for L'Oreal.
- Anything outside the SOP that the agent can't confidently resolve.

### Required disclaimers (work into responses where applicable)
- "Readings are for entertainment and reflection — they're not a substitute
  for medical, legal, or psychiatric advice."
- "The on-site ADHD/autism assessment is informational only — it's not a
  diagnosis, please follow up with your doctor."
- "Refunds on digital products are reviewed case-by-case for access
  issues only."
